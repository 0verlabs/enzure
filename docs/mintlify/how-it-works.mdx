Every statement in Enzure, called an **Assertion**, goes through the same steps. Most are never disputed and settle as true once their dispute window closes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Post">
    You post a statement (the **Claim**) with a NEAR bond and a dispute window (**Liveness**). The text is stored off-chain; only its hash goes on-chain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Dispute">
    Anyone who thinks the Claim is false can dispute it during Liveness by posting the same bond. If nobody does, skip to Settle.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rule">
    A NEAR AI model running in a TEE reads the Claim and both sides' Evidence and answers TRUE, FALSE or UNRESOLVABLE. The contract accepts the answer only if it can verify the model's signature.

    The losing side can then appeal to the DAO. UNRESOLVABLE, two different verdicts, or no verdict in time also go to the DAO. The DAO's ruling is final.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Settle">
    Once the last window closes, anyone calls `settle`. Bonds are credited to balances you withdraw, and the outcome is delivered to the contracts you named when posting.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who gets the bonds

| Case | Result |
| --- | --- |
| No dispute | The Asserter gets the bond back. |
| Disputed | The winner gets their bond back plus half the loser's. The DAO gets the other half. |
| Appealed | The appellant posts one more bond. Win, and you get it back. Lose, and it is split between the winner and the DAO. |
| The DAO doesn't rule in time | An appealed verdict stands and the appeal bond is returned. Otherwise every bond is returned and the outcome is FALSE. |

## Timers

The DAO sets these. The contract defaults are below; the testnet oracle uses much shorter ones so the demo is quick.

| Timer | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Liveness (dispute window) | 2 hours, or any value from 1 minute to 7 days |
| Time to get an AI Verdict | 1 hour |
| Appeal window | 24 hours |
| Time for the DAO to rule | 7 days |
