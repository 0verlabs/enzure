If you think a statement is false, dispute it before its dispute window closes. You post the same bond as the Asserter, and you can add your own Evidence.

## Dispute

```bash
near contract call-function as-transaction oracle-1794575543.enzure-council-a-1794575224.testnet \
  dispute json-args '{"assertion_id":7,"evidence_hash":null}' \
  prepaid-gas '100.0 Tgas' attached-deposit '1.01 NEAR' \
  sign-as <your-account>.testnet network-config testnet sign-with-keychain send
```

Attach the statement's bond plus 0.005 NEAR for storage. Upload Evidence first, as in [Post a statement](/guides/assert), and pass its hash as `evidence_hash`.

Next, a Resolver gets an AI Verdict and submits it. Anyone can be a Resolver; the [Keeper](/faq) does it automatically.

<Warning>
If the statement turns out to be true, you lose your bond.
</Warning>

## Appeal

If the AI Verdict goes against you, you can appeal once while the appeal window is open. Attach a bond equal to the statement's bond.

```bash
near contract call-function as-transaction oracle-1794575543.enzure-council-a-1794575224.testnet \
  appeal json-args '{"assertion_id":7}' \
  prepaid-gas '100.0 Tgas' attached-deposit '1 NEAR' \
  sign-as <your-account>.testnet network-config testnet sign-with-keychain send
```

The DAO rules with a normal Sputnik DAO proposal that calls `resolve_appeal`. Its ruling is final. If it doesn't rule in time, the AI Verdict stands and you get the appeal bond back.
