# Product brief

## Description
Enzure is an optimistic oracle on NEAR for the promises a contract can't check by itself (product page: https://0verlabs.com/enzure). A bonded statement settles as true unless someone disputes it. A TEE-signed AI Verdict rules on disputes, the losing side can appeal to a DAO, and outcomes are delivered to NEAR or EVM contracts.

Don't call it an "AI optimistic oracle". The AI Verdict is one feature, used only for disputes.

## Primary audience
Developers who want a contract to act on statements, and the people and agents who post and dispute them. (Assumption, not confirmed.)

## Jobs to be done
- Understand the four steps: post, dispute, rule, settle.
- Post, dispute, appeal, settle and withdraw.
- Receive outcomes in a NEAR or EVM contract.

## Style
Simplicity over everything. Short pages, plain words, few components. Follow the product page's wording.

## Status (2026-11-14)
NEAR testnet only. The Receiver on Base Sepolia, the web app and the Keeper are not deployed.
