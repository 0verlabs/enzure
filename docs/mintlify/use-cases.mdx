Any contract on NEAR or an EVM chain can use Enzure as a source of truth for a statement it can't check by itself. Each example below is a Claim someone could post.

## AI agents and escrow

Pay an agent only when its work matches the brief. This is the demo Enzure ships: the escrow pays the seller on TRUE and refunds the buyer on FALSE.

> "The deliverable in the Asserter's Evidence is a three-line poem about a lighthouse at night that uses the word "beam"."

## Prediction markets

Resolve real-world outcomes, such as elections, sports and milestones, without one party deciding.

> "The Kansas City Chiefs beat the Philadelphia Eagles in Super Bowl LVII."

## DeFi

Settle any condition, such as price thresholds, liquidations or option expiry, using general statements instead of narrow price feeds.

> "BTC/USD was below $55,000 at 00:00 UTC on March 15, 2024, per Coinbase OHLCV."

## Cross-chain verification

Confirm that something happened on another chain, then deliver the result to a contract on NEAR or any EVM chain with no bridge.

> "Address 0xA1b2...F3d4 sent 1.0 ETH on Ethereum mainnet at block 19,204,811."

## Insurance and risk

Trigger payouts from real-world events, such as flight delays, weather or protocol exploits.

> "Singapore Airlines flight SQ321 was delayed more than 3 hours on March 12, 2024."

## Real-world assets

Verify off-chain valuations, ownership transfers or compliance checks for tokenized assets.

> "The U.S. Treasury 6-month bill yield was 5.27% at auction on March 11, 2024."

<Note>
Only the escrow has example code today. The others use the same [Hooks](/developers/integration). Write Claims that a third party could check from the text and Evidence alone; vague Claims go to the DAO.
</Note>
